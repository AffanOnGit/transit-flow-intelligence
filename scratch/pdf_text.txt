3 Task 3 — Process Discovery & Interactive GUI
35 Marks
Objective
Discover a process map from the event logs and present it in an interactive graphical user interface (GUI) that
supports filtering and rich annotations.
Requirements
3a — Process Map Generation (10 marks)
• Use a process discovery algorithm on the merged event log.
• The map must show all routes connected, with nodes representing stops and directed edges representing
transitions.
• Edge labels must display the transition duration between consecutive stops.
3b — Route Filter (10 marks)
• Provide a dropdown or selector to filter the process map by route_id.
• Selecting FR-01 must display only the sub-graph for that route.
• Selecting All Routes must display the full connected map.
3c — Transition Duration on Edges (10 marks)
• Each directed edge must display the transition time between consecutive stops.
• Format: PAEC → IMC = 1min45sec (derived from 10:15:05−10:13:20).
• This is the only required annotation on edges. Additional analytics (throughput, bottleneck info, etc.)
may be displayed elsewhere in the GUI (e.g. a side panel, tooltip, or summary table) — not necessarily on
the edges.
3d — Case Frequency Display (5 marks)
• Display the number of trips (cases) that traversed each transition.
• This may be shown on edges, in tooltips, or in a separate panel — any clear presentation is acceptable.

5 Task 5 — Agentic AI Trip Planner
15 Marks
Objective
Modern process mining platforms such as Celonis demonstrate how agentic AI can be embedded directly into
a business process context, allowing users to query, navigate, and act on live process data through natural
language. In this task, you will replicate this idea at the route level: integrate a conversational AI agent into
yourGUIthatanswersnatural-languagetrip-planningqueriesgroundedinthediscoveredCDAroutedata. The
choice of model, API, or framework is entirely up to you.
Requirements
• Integrate an AI agent that has access to the extracted routes.csv and the discovered process map.
• The agent must be capable of handling queries such as:
– “I have to travel from Khanna Pul to NUST Metro Station — what are my options?”
– “Which route goes through Faizabad?”
– “What time does the last bus leave from H-9?”
– “How long does it take to get from Khanna Pul to Faizabad?”
– “Do any routes connect G-9 Markaz to F-10 Markaz?”
• Theagentshouldreturn: availableroute(s)andrequiredtransfers,estimatedtraveltimebasedondiscovered
transition durations, and the next available departure time.
• Responses must be grounded in the route data — the agent must not hallucinate stops.
• A chat interface panel must be embedded in the main GUI.
Example Interaction
User: I have to travel from Khanna Pul to NUST Metro Station. What are my options?
Agent: Basedonthecurrentschedule,RouteFR-02stopsatKhannaPul (dep.10:20)andreachesNUST
Metro Station in approximately 18 minutes (avg). Next departure: 10:35.
